# Grenade for Minecraft Bedrock

A throwable grenade that damages creatures but leaves your blocks alone. It flies in a slow, visible arc trailing sparks and smoke, so you can see where it will land.

> **Status: Test version.** Things may still change before the public release, so back up any world you care about. Download: [Releases page](https://github.com/Teliendan/mc_be_grenade/releases).

**Project page:** [teliendan.com](https://teliendan.com/mods/grenade/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=profile&utm_content=readme) · **Wiki:** [the grenade in detail](https://teliendan.com/wiki/grenade/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=profile&utm_content=readme)

## Features

- **Throw:** right-click to throw. The grenade explodes a moment after it lands, with a green puff, and works underwater too.
- **Blast:** hurts creatures within about 6 blocks, less with distance and behind cover. It does not break blocks, start fires or destroy items on the ground.
- **Friendly fire:** the blast hurts you, other players, pets and villagers as well, up to more than a full health bar at the centre. Throw it well away from yourself and your friends.
- **Your kills:** a grenade kill counts as yours, so you get the XP and the drops that only a player kill gives.
- **Dispensers:** dispensers fire grenades too. Their kills give no player-kill rewards.
- **Crafting:** in a crafting table, place four copper nuggets in a plus shape with one gunpowder in the middle. Grenades stack to 16, with a short cooldown between throws.
- **Vibrant Visuals:** with Vibrant Visuals on, the grenade has a painted-metal look, with a shiny steel pin and lever.
- **Languages:** English, Czech, German, French, Polish, Spanish (Spain and Mexico) and Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil). The translations are AI drafts; corrections are welcome.

## Install

1. Download the `.mcaddon` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/Teliendan/mc_be_grenade/releases).
2. Open the file with Minecraft on Windows, Android or iPhone/iPad; it imports both packs.
3. In the world settings, turn on the Grenade **behavior pack** (its resource pack comes with it). New and existing worlds both work.

Xbox, PlayStation and Switch can't import add-on files directly, but console players can join a world that already has it.

Requirements: tested on Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.63. No experimental toggles are needed. It works on its own and needs none of my other add-ons.
